Harry hesitated for a moment, then pulled his scarf from around his face and, ignoring Ron’s

As they followed Hermione and Leanne up the road, Harry was thinking furiously. They had just

“Malfoy knows about this necklace. It was in a case at Borgin and Burkes four years ago, I saw
