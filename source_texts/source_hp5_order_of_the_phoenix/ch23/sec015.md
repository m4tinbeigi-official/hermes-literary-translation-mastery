“I’ve used it against him,” said Harry quietly. “It saved my life in June.” 
 
Smith opened his mouth stupidly. The rest of the room was very quiet. 
 
“But if you think it’s beneath you, you can leave,” Harry said. 
 
Smith did not move. Nor did anybody else. 
 
“Okay,” said Harry, his mouth slightly drier than usual with all these eyes upon him, “I reckon 
we should all divide into pairs and practice.” 
 
It felt very odd to be issuing instructions, but not nearly as odd as seeing them followed.
