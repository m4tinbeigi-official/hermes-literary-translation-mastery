“Course we did,” said Hagrid, “we hadn’ gone all that way ter give up after two days! We wen’

Hermione clapped her hands to her mouth. 
 
“How did you get out of that?” asked Harry. 
 
“Wouldn’ta done if Olympe hadn’ bin there,” said Hagrid. “She pulled out her wand an’ did 
some o’ the fastes’ spellwork I’ve ever seen. Ruddy marvellous. Hit the two holdin’ me right in 
the eyes with Conjunctivitus Curses an’ they dropped me straightaway - bu’ we were in trouble 
then, ‘cause we’d used magic against ‘em, an’ that’s what giants hate abou’ wizards. We had ter 
leg it an’ we knew there was no way we was going ter be able ter march inter the camp again.”
