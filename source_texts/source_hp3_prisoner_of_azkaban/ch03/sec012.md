He gave another bow and left. 
 
      Harry sat on his bed for a long time, absentmindedly stroking Hedwig. The sky outside the 
window was changing rapidly from deep, velvety blue to cold, steely gray and then, slowly, to 
pink shot with gold. Harry could hardly believe that he’d left Privet Drive only a few hours ago, 
that he wasn’t expelled, and that he was now facing two completely Dursley-free weeks. 
 
      “It’s been a very weird night, Hedwig,” he yawned. 
 
      And without even removing his glasses, he slumped back onto his pillows and fell asleep.
