# Literary Deep Analysis & Companion Guide

## Overview
A comprehensive literary dissection and critical analysis engine. Analyzes the underlying architecture of fiction: archetypal symbolism, narrative pacing, moral dilemmas, thematic tension, foreshadowing setups/payoffs, and character psychological transformation.

## Analysis Frameworks

### 1. Thematic & Moral Argument Dissection
- **Central Theme:** What universal truth or human question is the story examining? (e.g. Choice vs. Fate; Death as the next great adventure; Power and its corruptive nature).
- **Thematic Counter-Argument:** How does the antagonist or opposing force challenge the protagonist's worldview?

### 2. Foreshadowing & Narrative Seeds (Chekhov's Guns)
Identify and track narrative clues:
- **Seed Planted:** Subtle mention in early chapters (e.g. Sirius Black's flying motorbike in Chapter 1).
- **Misdirection (Red Herring):** Diverting reader suspicion (e.g. Snape's sinister behavior masking Quirrell's true motives).
- **Payoff / Harvest:** The moment the seed explodes into narrative significance.

### 3. Archetypal & Mythological Underpinnings
- **Joseph Campbell / Carl Jung Archetypes:** The Hero, Shadow, Mentor, Threshold Guardian, Shapeshifter, Trickster.
- **Symbolic Motifs:** Recurrent colors (Gold vs. Green), mirrors (Erised), boundaries (Forbidden Forest), rebirth symbols (Phoenix).

### 4. Psychological Character Arc Mapping
Map the internal journey along 4 stages:
1. **The Lie Believed:** The flawed belief the character clings to at the start.
2. **The First Fracture:** The event that proves the lie is inadequate.
3. **The Crucible (Dark Night):** Forced to choose between the comfortable Lie and painful Truth.
4. **Self-Realization & Change:** Climax action made possible only by embracing the Truth.

## Output Products

- **Executive Literary Review (گزارش کالبدشکافی ادبی):** 5-page structured analysis of narrative strengths, weaknesses, and emotional resonance.
- **Book Club Discussion Guide (راهنمای گفت‌وگو و حلقه کتاب‌خوانی):** 10 deep, non-obvious questions exploring character ethics and moral grey areas.
- **Etymological & Linguistic Breakdown:** Origins of character names, locations, and invented jargon.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Clear distinction between plot events (what happens) and theme/subtext (what it means).
- [ ] Every character arc mapped with internal flaw, realization, and behavioral change.
- [ ] Foreshadowing table links every seed to its final narrative payoff.
- [ ] Stylistic analysis evaluates prose rhythm, cadence, and tone consistency.
